Popover

A small floating panel anchored to its trigger. It keeps itself on screen and closes when you are done.

Anything goes in here.

Popovers keep themselves on screen, even next to the window edge.

How to use

nc-popover is a small floating panel next to its trigger, for extra information or a few controls. Put a button in slot="trigger". It is not modal: the page stays usable, and it closes on Escape, an outside click, or when focus leaves. It also opens and closes through [(open)] or show(), hide() and toggle().

Accessibility

  • The trigger button gets aria-expanded and aria-controls automatically. Add an ariaLabel to announce the panel as a dialog.
  • Animations stop with reduced motion, and Windows high contrast mode draws borders.
